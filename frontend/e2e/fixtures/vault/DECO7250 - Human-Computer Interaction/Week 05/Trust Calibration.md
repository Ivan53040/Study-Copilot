# Trust Calibration

Calibrated trust means people's reliance on an AI system matches what the system can actually do.

## Why it matters

- Over-trust leads to automation bias: accepting recommendations without enough scrutiny.
- Under-trust leads to disuse, even when the system would help.

## How interfaces support it

1. Communicate confidence and limitations in plain language.
2. Show evidence or provenance, see [[Explainable AI]].
3. Make hand-off points visible and allow correction.
